About ICEI

A place for learning, growth and purpose.

ICEI is the education department of Dawat-e-Islami. It works to support educational opportunity while encouraging students to develop knowledge, confidence and strong personal values.

With a vision that reaches across India, ICEI brings its educational mission to communities and learners in a spirit of service.
